# Code of Conduct

STSB discussions must remain evidence-oriented, respectful, and safe for participation.

## Expected behavior

- Challenge claims and models, not people.
- Distinguish verified facts, hypotheses, and preferences.
- Provide reproducible evidence when making technical claims.
- Credit prior work and contributors accurately.
- Accept correction and document changed conclusions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate deception, doxxing, publishing private information, sustained personal attacks, and attempts to manipulate or intimidate contributors are not permitted.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove violating contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions should identify the violated rule without exposing unnecessary personal information.

Report sensitive conduct concerns privately through the repository's security-advisory channel. Ordinary technical disagreement belongs in the relevant public issue.
