# Governance

## Status

STSB is an open pre-standard. The repository owner is the initial maintainer and release steward. This is an operational fact, not evidence that the taxonomy is correct.

## Decision classes

- Editorial changes require one maintainer review.
- Schema-compatible additions require tests and a written rationale.
- Root additions, removals, or merges require a public counterexample, impact analysis, and a versioned decision record.
- Breaking changes require a new schema version and migration notes.

## Acceptance rule

A proposal is accepted only when its evidence is reproducible and its distinction cannot be represented by existing composition. Popularity and authority are not substitutes for evidence.

## Conflicts

Potential conflicts of interest must be disclosed in the relevant issue. Decisions remain inspectable in Git history and issue discussions.

## Path to shared governance

After two independent implementations and three recurring external contributors, maintainers should propose a small technical steering group with published membership, terms, voting rules, and removal rules.
